How an engagement runs

A process you can follow and verify.

Four steps, in order. You will know which step your matter is in at all times.

  1. 01

    Consult

    We understand your records, obligations and deadline in a first call. You learn what is required, what it costs and who will do the work.

  2. 02

    Engage

    A written engagement letter fixes scope, deliverables, fees and timelines. Nothing is done outside it without your approval.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Work proceeds on the agreed calendar. You receive drafts, queries and reports in writing, with every figure traceable to a source.

  4. 04

    Stand behind

    After delivery, we respond to notices, follow-ups and reviews until the matter is closed. Corrections, when needed, are made without argument.
