Why join us

What an internship here actually looks like.

No photocopying runs, no watching. Interns are put on engagements with a named supervisor and a set of skills to build by the end of the stint.

  • 01

    Real working papers

    You draft audit programmes, reconcile ledgers and prepare working papers under review — the same files the principal signs.

  • 02

    A named supervisor

    Every intern reports to one engagement manager. Progress is reviewed on a fixed schedule, in writing, so you always know where you stand.

  • 03

    Skills, not errands

    You leave with a demonstrable skill set: statutory filing, NFRS/ASN mapping, taxation basics and audit documentation — usable on your next application.

  • 04

    A reference that names results

    Interns who complete the program receive a written reference describing the work they actually delivered, not a generic letter.
